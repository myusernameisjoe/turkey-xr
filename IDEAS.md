# Turkey Chase ideas

Every idea for the game goes here, even silly ones. Move an idea up to **In progress** when we start
building it, and to **Done** when it's on the live game.

Play it: https://myusernameisjoe.github.io/turkey-xr/

## Done

- **Turkey chase**: a turkey wanders and pecks, runs away when you get close, gets tired, and you tag it. It gobbles so you can find it by ear.
- **Two places to play**: AR (your real backyard through passthrough) and VR (a fake yard).
- **Easy mode** (`?easy`): slower turkey, tag it without crouching.
- **Joystick movement** in the fake yard, with smooth turning. `?snap` turns in steps, `?invert` flips forward/back.
- **Item Board**: press A or X, look at a button, and click to swap items.
- **Unlocks by catches**: catch turkeys to earn items. The new item goes straight into your hand.
- **Wet Noodle** (1 catch): swing it to bonk the turkey dizzy, then grab it.
- **Fresh start every game**: catches reset each time the page loads. `?save` keeps them.

## In progress
